Double-Entry Accounting Principles Enforced

  • Inventory Asset: Book purchases are recorded as inventory assets at cost on the balance sheet and are not expensed immediately upon purchase.
  • COGS Recognition: Cost of Goods Sold is recognized strictly when each book is sold, matched against the unit purchase cost.
  • Provisional vs Confirmed: When a book's purchase cost is unknown (NULL), gross profit is marked as provisional until physical cost reconciliation.
  • Owner Contributions: Funds contributed by Lamis are tracked as equity additions, never confused with customer sales revenue.
  • Zero Floating-Point Errors: All calculations in Algerian Dinars (DA) are conducted with exact integer arithmetic.
